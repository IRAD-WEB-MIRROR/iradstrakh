Accident insurance

Insurance against accidents: payment in case of injury, disability or death as a result of an accident.

KindPremiumSum insured, som
Personal accident, adults, 24 hours0.8% of the sum insured5,000 to 100,000
Schoolchildren, for the school year (1 Sep – 31 May)25 som per pupil5,000
Athletes and sports sections1.5% of the sum insured5,000 to 50,000
Passengers of buses and taxis (compulsory for licensed carriers)30 som per seat a year20,000 per seat

Payments

Consequence of the accidentPayment
Death100% of the sum insured
Disability, group I90%
Disability, group II75%
Disability, group III50%
Fractures, burns, other injuriesby the table of the Rules, 2–30%
Temporary loss of working capacity (from the 8th day)0.2% for each day, not more than 60 days

Passengers

Licensed bus, minibus and taxi operators must insure every seat stated in the technical passport, with a sum insured of not less than 20,000 som per seat, for the whole period of the licence. The policy number is entered in the licence application on e-Irad. A licence lapses on the day the policy lapses.
