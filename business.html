For business

Iradstrakh insures the property of the state enterprises of the Zone, pledged property, cargo and the liability of businesses, and provides group medical insurance for staff.

Kind of insuranceWhat is insuredPrice
Property of state enterprises (compulsory)Buildings, equipment and stock of the Zone's enterprisesby contract, through the State Procurement Portal
