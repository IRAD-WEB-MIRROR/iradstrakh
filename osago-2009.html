COMPULSORY MOTOR INSURANCE FROM 1 APRIL 2009!

Dear vehicle owners! According to the Compulsory Motor Third-Party Insurance Act 2009 (№ 09-15) from 01.04.2009 every vehicle registered in the Zone must have the policy of compulsory insurance of civil liability of vehicle owners. Driving without the policy is punished by fine.

Policies are sold in all offices of Iradstrakh and at additional counters in vehicle registration offices (Talas, Nurzhan). Do not wait for the last days!
