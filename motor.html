Compulsory motor insurance

Compulsory insurance of the civil liability of vehicle owners (OSAGO) under the Compulsory Motor Third-Party Insurance Act 2009 (№ 09-15). Every vehicle registered in the Zone must have a valid policy. Iradstrakh is the insurer for compulsory motor policies in the Zone.

What is covered

Harm caused by your vehicle to the life, health and property of other people (third parties). Damage to your own vehicle is not covered by the compulsory policy.

Vehicles entering the Zone

Vehicles registered in Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan or Uzbekistan buy a short-term (border) policy at the Talas North Terminal office (daily 8:00–20:00) and from our agent at the Chu Gate crossing. Payment in som or in US dollars at the official rate of the Bank of Irad.

How to buy

  • Come to any of our offices or the counter in the vehicle registration office of the traffic police.
  • Bring the technical passport of the vehicle and your identity card (for an organisation: power of attorney).
  • The policy is issued in 10 minutes and entered in the register the same day; the register is shown on e-Irad within five working days.
  • Keep the policy in the vehicle. The Highway Patrol Corps may ask to see it.
Lost your policy? A duplicate is issued at the office where it was bought, on presentation of the identity card. Duplicate: 15 som.
